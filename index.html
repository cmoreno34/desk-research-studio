Desk research in 2 checks

Estudios de Mercado I · Tema 2 — Desk research worksheet
Case. FreshlyCosmetics wants to launch a natural facial anti-aging line in Spain. In two quick checks — the market size and the trends — decide if it's worth a €8,000 survey and what that survey should ask. Another country? Swap the terms and use your own national statistics office.

1 How big is the market?

Find one credible figure for the market's size and how fast it grows.

Open a search ↗
Do this:
  1. Open 2 results (prefer a report or an association — not a blog).
  2. Find the market value (€ or $) and its growth % (CAGR).
  3. Write it below with where it's from and the year.

2 What's trending?

See what people search for — it shows what they want, right now.

Open Google Trends (ready) ↗
Do this:
  1. It opens comparing natural cosmetics · anti-ageing · retinol. Set your country (top left).
  2. See which line is rising.
  3. Scroll down to “Related queries → Rising” and copy 3 of them below.
