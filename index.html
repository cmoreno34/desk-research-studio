A Source log

    
One row per source. Record the fieldwork date and the definition, keep the link, and score each 1–5 (reliability · representativeness · currency — see 2.2).

B Which source would you trust?

    
From the warm-up (Kantar study / beauty blog / Brand X release): which ONE figure do you put in front of the client, and what do you tell them about the other two?

    
  


  

    
C Findings — 3 to 5, each a full sentence

    
Each finding is a sentence backed by 2+ sources. Not “Market size” but “The natural anti-aging category is small but growing 4× faster than the mass market it takes share from.”
